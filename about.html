About Korean Steps 🐥

Korean Steps is a free app that teaches Korean the way it actually builds — one small step at a time. You start by reading Hangul, combine sounds into words, and grow into full sentences and real conversations: 1,000 bite-size lessons in 100 units, structured by TOPIK levels 1–4 (A1–B2).

How it teaches

  • Whole lessons, two voices — every lesson is narrated by two voices, so you hear each Korean sound modeled more than one way.
  • A quiz after every lesson — TOPIK-style questions that check meaning, listening and typing, not just recognition.
  • Picture wordbook — 498 illustrated words with pronunciation and audio, plus a catalog of 11,000+ course words and phrases.
  • Games that drill — Word Speed, Typing Game, Falling Words and more, with weekly leaderboards and medals.
  • Level tests & TOPIK mock exams — scored only at the end, just like the real test.
  • A new daily goal every day, coins, outfits for your chick, and 10 ranks from Peasant to Emperor.

Learn anywhere

The full course lives in the free Android app — it works offline after the first load, and Google sign-in syncs your progress across devices. You can also start learning right here in your browser, full screen, on the Lessons page.

Korean Steps is available in English, Spanish and Portuguese.
